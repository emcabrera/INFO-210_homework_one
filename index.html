About Me

I am in my 3rd year for Game Design & Development. When I'm not in school, I like to draw, read, play video games, my favorites so far is Tomodachi Life and Animal Crossing, and bake sweets. I've been drawing since I was in middle school and started doing digital drawing in my sophomore year when a friend introduced me to it, while I don't believe that I'm as good as those I've seen on social media, I hope to get there one day, until then I always aim to improve and learn new tips and tricks. My favorite color is pink, it used to be purple because I thought it was too girly, later on I ended up loving the color again.
